## PROJECT OVERVIEW

Artificial intelligence is changing how organizations understand customers, optimize operations, analyze information, and make strategic decisions.

However, successful AI adoption begins with identifying the right business problem.

This project explores how a fictional retail organization, **Horizon Retail**, can identify potential AI opportunities, evaluate business value, compare implementation feasibility, and develop a structured AI adoption strategy.

The objective is to connect business requirements with practical AI applications and establish a framework for making informed investment decisions.

## THE BUSINESS CHALLENGE

Retail organizations generate large amounts of information through sales, inventory operations, customer interactions, and purchasing behavior.

Turning this information into useful business decisions can be challenging.

Potential challenges include:

- Understanding changes in customer purchasing behavior.
- Forecasting future product demand.
- Improving the efficiency of customer support.
- Identifying opportunities for personalized recommendations.
- Evaluating which AI initiatives deserve investment first.

The central question is:

**Which AI opportunity should a business prioritize to achieve meaningful business value while managing cost, feasibility, and risk?**

---

## PROJECT OBJECTIVES

The project aims to establish a structured approach to AI strategy.

1. Identify potential AI applications relevant to retail.
2. Define criteria for comparing candidate initiatives.
3. Evaluate potential business impact and implementation feasibility.
4. Consider costs, operational requirements, and risks.
5. Prioritize a suitable AI pilot based on the analysis.
6. Develop a phased implementation roadmap.
7. Identify appropriate performance indicators.
8. Incorporate responsible AI considerations into the recommendation.

## AI OPPORTUNITY AREAS

### 01. Demand Forecasting

Predictive analytics can help estimate future product demand using historical sales and relevant business information.

**Potential business value:** Better-informed inventory and purchasing decisions.

### 02. Intelligent Customer Support

Natural language processing can help interpret customer questions and retrieve answers from approved information sources.

**Potential business value:** More efficient handling of routine customer enquiries.

### 03. Customer Analytics

Customer segmentation and behavioral analysis can help identify patterns in purchasing activity.

**Potential business value:** Better-informed customer engagement and marketing decisions.

### 04. Product Recommendation Systems

Recommendation algorithms can identify potentially relevant products using suitable purchase and interaction data.

**Potential business value:** Improved product discovery and personalization.

*These are example opportunities. The final recommendation should be based on the evidence and results documented in the project notebook.*
